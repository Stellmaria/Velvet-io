Privacy Policy

Privacy at Velvet Anatomy.

This policy explains how Velvet Anatomy processes information when its owners use the private application and its Pinterest publishing integration.

Effective: August 10, 2026

Service: Velvet Anatomy

Scope: Private archive and connected publishing integrations

1. Scope

Velvet Anatomy is a private content-management and publishing application operated for its owners. This Privacy Policy covers information processed by Velvet Anatomy in connection with the private application and connected third-party publishing services, including Pinterest.

The public website is informational only. It does not provide public access to the private archive or administrative controls.

2. Information we process

Depending on the connected features used by an owner, Velvet Anatomy may process:

  • media and related archive metadata selected by an owner for review or publication;
  • technical identifiers needed to retrieve the selected media from the private archive;
  • authenticated Pinterest account information made available by the Pinterest API as required for publishing;
  • Pinterest board and Pin information needed to display available destinations and create Pins;
  • OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, client credentials, or equivalent authorization material required for a configured integration;
  • limited operational logs used to diagnose application and provider errors.

Velvet Anatomy does not ask owners to provide their Pinterest password or Pinterest session cookies.

3. How information is used

Information is processed only as needed to operate Velvet Anatomy and its owner-requested publishing workflows, including to:

  • display the boards available to the authenticated Pinterest account;
  • prepare an owner-selected archive image for publication;
  • create a Pin on the board chosen and confirmed by the owner;
  • report whether a publication action succeeded or failed;
  • maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the private application.

4. Authentication and security

Pinterest access is performed through Pinterest-supported API authentication. Pinterest credentials and tokens are kept on the application server or its configured secret-management environment and are not intentionally exposed in Telegram callback data, public pages, source-controlled configuration values, or user-visible provider error messages.

Operational error handling is designed to avoid logging authorization headers, raw credentials, and secret values. No security method can guarantee absolute protection, but Velvet Anatomy limits credential exposure and access to the integration to its owners and authorized application processes.

5. Sharing and sale of information

Velvet Anatomy does not sell Pinterest data or use it to build advertising profiles. Information is shared with third-party services only when necessary to perform an owner-requested function, such as sending an approved image and Pin metadata to Pinterest through the Pinterest API.

Infrastructure and hosting providers may process limited technical information as necessary to operate the application, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

6. Data retention

Pinterest authorization material is retained only while the integration is configured and required for the application to access the connected account. Owners can revoke Pinterest access through Pinterest or remove the corresponding integration configuration.

Board and Pin API responses are used for the publishing workflow. Velvet Anatomy may retain limited operational records required for application reliability, security, and troubleshooting, but those records are designed not to contain raw authorization credentials.

7. Owner choices and access revocation

Owners can stop Pinterest processing by disabling or disconnecting the Pinterest integration. They may also revoke the application's Pinterest authorization using the controls provided by Pinterest.

After authorization is revoked, Velvet Anatomy can no longer use the revoked credentials to access the Pinterest account.

8. Third-party services

Pinterest is a separate service with its own privacy policy and terms. When an owner instructs Velvet Anatomy to publish content to Pinterest, the information sent to Pinterest is also processed by Pinterest under Pinterest's applicable terms and privacy practices.

9. Children's privacy

Velvet Anatomy is an owner-operated private application and is not directed to children. The public informational website does not provide account registration or a public user service.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Velvet Anatomy changes its connected services, data-processing practices, or legal and operational requirements. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests related to Velvet Anatomy can be submitted to the project owner through the public website repository: github.com/Stellmaria/Velvet-io.
